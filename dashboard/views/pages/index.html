Feature Set

Built for servers
that mean business

Roco packs years of community feedback into a single focused bot. No bloat, no unnecessary complexity — just the tools your server actually needs, executed with precision and reliability you can count on every single day.

Anti-Link System

Automatically detect and remove unauthorized links and Discord invite URLs the moment they are posted. Configure channel whitelists, role exceptions, and automatic punishment escalation — all without lifting a finger once it's set up.

Free

Complete Audit Logs

15+ distinct log categories capture every meaningful Discord event in granular detail. Message edits and deletions, role grants and removals, channel creations, voice movements, invite usage — all logged, timestamped, and beautifully formatted.

Free

Message Sniping

Recover deleted and edited messages instantly with a single command. Full original message content, author details, timestamp, and attachment references are all preserved and retrievable — perfect for catching rule-breakers who delete their tracks.

Free

Log Backup

Your audit history is far too important to lose. Roco automatically backs up all log data off-channel so even if your entire log category gets wiped, every single record remains safely intact and fully recoverable by your admin team.

Premium

Smart Automod

Rule-based automod that escalates punishments intelligently based on repeat offences. Warn, mute, kick, or ban — with fully configurable thresholds, cooldowns, and bypass permissions per role. Every action is logged for staff review.

Free

Channel Lockdown

Instantly lock individual channels, entire categories, or your whole server in emergency situations with a single command. Full write-access restoration is just as fast — complete situational control without touching a single permission manually.

Free

Welcome & Goodbye

Roll out the red carpet for every new arrival with richly formatted welcome embeds, custom background banners, DM greetings, and auto-assigned join roles. When members leave, send a heartfelt farewell to any channel you choose.

Free

Giveaways

Host feature-packed giveaways with role requirements, entry blacklists, bonus entry multipliers, and fully automatic winner selection. Re-roll winners instantly, end early, or pause mid-giveaway — total host control throughout.

Free

Confessions

Give your community a private, anonymous space to speak freely. Confessions are posted publicly without identifying information while an admin-only hidden log quietly records the author — balancing freedom and safety.

Free

Booster Rewards

Automatically celebrate and reward members the instant they boost your server. Assign exclusive booster roles, fire a custom thank-you message to any channel, and even DM the booster personally — all completely hands-free.

Free

Suggestions Board

Give your community a structured space to share feedback and ideas. Members submit suggestions that Roco formats into clean embeds with upvote and downvote reactions. Staff can approve, deny, or mark as in-progress.

Free

Sticky Messages

Pin messages that always stay pinned at the very bottom of a channel regardless of conversation speed. Essential for displaying rules, important announcements, or key links in high-traffic channels where standard pins get buried.

Free

YouTube Alerts

Get notified in Discord the instant your favourite YouTube channels publish a new upload or go live. Supports tracking multiple channels simultaneously, each with fully custom embed messages, role pings, and thumbnails.

Premium

Twitch Live Alerts

Real-time stream-start notifications appear the moment a tracked streamer goes live — complete with live viewer counts, current game, stream title, and a direct link. Track unlimited streamers with individual settings per entry.

Premium

Change Log Posting

Keep your community informed and genuinely excited about updates with a built-in changelog management system. Format and dispatch version updates to a dedicated channel in seconds with custom embeds and version tags.

Free

Autoresponder

Set unlimited keyword or phrase triggers that Roco responds to automatically with customisable embed messages, plain text replies, or emoji reactions. Supports exact match, partial match, and starts-with modes.

Free

Reaction Roles

Let members self-assign roles by reacting to messages. Supports toggle mode, exclusive mode for single-role-per-group, and open multi-role mode. Completely button-based with zero commands required from members after setup.

Free

Uptime Monitor

Continuous uptime pinging with automatic latency spike alerts pushed to a dedicated log channel. Know the instant anything goes wrong — before your members even notice. Detailed reports and graphs available in the dashboard.

Premium

Fun & Games

Keep your community entertained with a full arsenal of fun commands. UwU mode, 8-ball predictions, coinflips, trivia quizzes, roast generators, and much more absurdity to lighten the mood and bring your community closer together.

Free

Full Ticket System

A complete support ticket ecosystem built natively into Discord. Create categorised ticket panels, define staff roles, configure auto-responses, and let members open tickets with a single button press. Scales from small communities to busy professional support operations.

Free

Ticket Management

Claim tickets, assign them to specific staff, add or remove users mid-conversation, rename channels, and close with a detailed HTML transcript automatically saved and delivered. Re-open, archive, or escalate through intuitive Discord buttons.

Free

Button-Based Actions

Every single interaction inside Roco's ticket system is driven by intuitive Discord buttons — no slash commands, no syntax to memorise. Your staff and members operate through clean, native Discord controls anyone can use from their very first ticket.

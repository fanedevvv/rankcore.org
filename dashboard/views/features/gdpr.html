Data Deletion

This removes your request log entries from our database. Deleted data cannot be recovered. To fully erase your data under GDPR/CCPA, contact support@rankcore.org.

You can also manage your privacy via Discord using the /privacy command with subcommands delete, stop, and resume.

IP Verification Data

IP Verification stores encrypted records of your IP address, risk assessment data, and guild membership history. Deleting this data will unverify you in all servers where you have completed IP verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Custom Branding mean?

Custom Branding gives your server its own dedicated bot — a custom name, avatar, embed colours, and status. It runs on an isolated dedicated server so crashes or restarts never affect other bots. Available as an add-on for any plan, or included with Elite.

Can I upgrade my whole server instead of just myself?

Yes! During checkout, select your Discord server from the dropdown. All members in that server will automatically gain access to your plan's features — they don't need their own subscription.

Can I cancel at any time?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from the Manage button on your Subscriptions page. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes — you can switch plans at any time. Upgrades are prorated immediately, and downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record — RankCore never sees or stores your card details, and VAT invoices are sent to you automatically.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer refunds within 3 days of initial purchase if you're unhappy. Reach out in our support server and we'll sort it out.

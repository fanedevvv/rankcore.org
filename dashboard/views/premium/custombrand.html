Overview
What is Custom Branding?
Custom Branding lets you run your own Discord bot — powered by RankCore — under your own name, avatar, and presence. Your server members see your bot, not RankCore.
Your bot runs as a fully isolated process. Crashes do not affect RankCore or other branded bots.
Step 1
Create a Discord Application
1
Go to discord.com/developers/applications → New Application.
2
Click Bot in the sidebar → Reset Token. Copy the token.
⚠️ Never share your bot token. Treat it like a password.
Step 1 cont.
Enable Privileged Intents
On the Bot page, enable all three under Privileged Gateway Intents:
✓
Presence Intent
✓
Server Members Intent
✓
Message Content Intent
⚠️ Missing intents will cause an "intents" error on startup.
Step 2
Invite the Bot to Your Server
1
Developer Portal → OAuth2 → URL Generator.
2
Scopes: bot + applications.commands.
3
Bot Permissions: Administrator.
4
Invite to this server only.
Step 3
Submit Your Token
Paste the token and click Start Bot. Your token is encrypted at rest and never exposed via any API.

Troubleshooting
Common Errors
!
Invalid token — regenerate in the Developer Portal.
!
Missing Intents — re-check all three are enabled.
!
Bot not responding — ensure you invited with applications.commands + Administrator.
